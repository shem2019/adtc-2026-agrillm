  Explain to a farmer with no formal training why rotating maize with beans
  improves soil fertility. Keep it practical and under 150 words.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  BASE   (11.2s, 227 words)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Rotating maize with beans in a farmer's field can significantly improve soil
  fertility in several practical ways:

  1. **Nitrogen Fixation**: Beans fix atmospheric nitrogen into a form that plants
  can use. This reduces the need for synthetic nitrogen fertilizers, which are
  expensive and can leach into groundwater.

  2. **Improved Soil Structure**: Beans help break up compacted soil, allowing for
  better water infiltration and root growth. This leads to healthier plants and
  more robust soil structure.

  3. **Increased Organic Matter**: Beans add organic matter to the soil, which
  improves its structure and fertility. This organic matter also helps retain
  moisture and nutrients.

  4. **Reduced Pests and Diseases**: The presence of beans in the rotation can
  deter pests and diseases that might otherwise thrive in maize fields. This
  reduces the need for chemical pesticides and fungicides.

  5. **Nutrient Cycling**: By rotating crops, nutrients are cycled back into the
  soil, reducing the need for continuous input of nutrients. This helps maintain
  soil fertility over time.

  6. **Water Management**: Beans help manage water by reducing soil erosion and
  improving water retention. This can lead to more efficient use of water
  resources.

  7. **Biodiversity**: Including beans in the rotation increases biodiversity in
  the field, which can lead to a more resilient and productive farm system.

  By implementing this rotation, farmers can enhance soil health, reduce input
  costs, and improve overall farm productivity.
